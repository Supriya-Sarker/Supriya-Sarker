## Hi, I'm Supriya Sarker

Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at the [University of Arkansas](https://www.uark.edu/) and Graduate Research Assistant. I build agentic AI, large language model (LLM), and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems for high-stakes engineering applications, including nuclear power plant maintenance and manufacturing quality inspection.

**Currently:** LLM and RAG frameworks for maintenance prioritization in nuclear power plants.

### Featured projects

- **[multi-agent-manufacturing-inspection](https://github.com/Supriya-Sarker/multi-agent-manufacturing-inspection)**: agentic LLM framework for multimodal in-situ weld quality assessment in wire arc additive manufacturing.
- **[beacon_dataset](https://github.com/Supriya-Sarker/beacon_dataset)**: Beacon, a real-world driving dataset recorded during blackouts, with reinforcement learning for traffic control (IEEE/RSJ IROS 2025).
- **[driving-maneuvers-classification](https://github.com/Supriya-Sarker/driving-maneuvers-classification-supervised-approach-)**: LSTM and transfer-learning methods for classifying driving maneuvers from time-series sensor data (IEEE Access 2021).

### Research interests

Agentic AI and LLMs · Multimodal AI for inspection and quality assessment · Reinforcement learning for control and decision-making
